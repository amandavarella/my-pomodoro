🍅 My Pomodoro

A Pomodoro timer that lives in the macOS menu bar.

Download for Mac

Latest release · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · All releases

What it does

  • Shows the remaining time in the menu bar, with options to hide the icon, the time, or the seconds when space is tight.
  • Configurable focus, short break, and long break lengths, and how many pomodoros come before the long break.
  • The alert plays on the MacBook speakers even when your audio goes through headphones, BlackHole, or a Multi-Output Device.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded DMG and drag Pomodoro into Applications.
  2. Open Pomodoro and look for the tomato in the menu bar. There is no Dock icon.

If macOS says it can't open Pomodoro

The app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Allow it once:

  • macOS 15 and later: open Pomodoro, click Done, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the Pomodoro message, and click Open Anyway.
  • macOS 13 and 14: Control-click Pomodoro in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.
  • Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pomodoro.app
